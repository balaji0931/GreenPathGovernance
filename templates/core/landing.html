Separation of Responsibilities

Strict Role-Based Multi-Tier Architecture

Each actor in the municipal waste ecosystem has an isolated workspace designed precisely for their operational mandate.

Tier 1

1. Super Admin HQ

Apex authority for Block / District level oversight. Provisions new Gram Panchayats, configures Wards, and appoints accredited Village Managers.

  • System-wide multi-village scope
  • Village Manager credentials
Tier 2

2. Village Manager

Operational supervisor of the Panchayat. Registers households, prints batch QR code tags, provisions waste collectors, and exports daily governance reports.

  • Household register & QR print
  • Daily ward completion analytics
Tier 3

3. Waste Collector

Field frontline worker. Uses phone camera to scan gate QR codes, automatically validating that the household belongs to their assigned Gram Panchayat.

  • Real-time gate scan authentication
  • Segregation rating & door-locked logs
Tier 4

4. Resident Household

Empowered citizen portal. Allows residents to confirm today's pickup time, view historical segregation audits, maintain green streaks, and reprint gate cards.

  • Zero cost to household forever
  • Streak & daily pickup verification
