Audit-Proof Workflow

How the Doorstep Cycle Works

Zero paper logs. Zero duplicate tallies. Real-time digital accountability.

1

QR Generation

Village manager registers the household and prints a unique QR tag containing zero personal details.

2

Doorstep Scan

Collector points smartphone camera to scan the tag. Server verifies village boundary and active status.

3

Segregation Audit

Collector inputs waste types (Wet, Dry, Sanitary), gives 1-5 star rating, or marks reason if door was locked.

4

Governance Reports

Daily metrics instantly update for the Village Manager and Gram Panchayat leaders with ward rankings.
