Household Solid Waste Segregation Guide

Segregating waste at source is the most critical pillar of Swachh Gram Panchayat operations. Under GreenPath governance, every household is audited on a 1-to-5 star scale based on source segregation.

Stream 1 (Green Bin)

Wet / Organic Waste

Biodegradable organic matter converted into agricultural compost or biogas at village composting beds.

Allowed Items:
  • Vegetable and fruit peels
  • Leftover cooked food & tea grounds
  • Garden dry leaves and twigs
  • Egg shells and floral waste
💡 Tip: Keep wet waste without plastic liners. Liquid should be drained before disposal.
Stream 2 (Blue Bin)

Dry / Recyclables

Non-biodegradable recyclable items routed to Material Recovery Facilities (MRF) or licensed recycling units.

Allowed Items:
  • Cardboard boxes & paper
  • Milk pouches & clean plastic containers
  • Metal cans & bottle caps
  • Glass bottles (unbroken)
💡 Tip: Rinse plastic pouches with a splash of water so food residue does not attract pests.
Stream 3 (Red / Marked Bag)

Sanitary & Hazardous

Infectious or toxic materials requiring specialized sanitary incineration or deep burial according to norms.

Allowed Items:
  • Sanitary pads & diapers
  • Used bandages & cotton swabs
  • Expired medicines & ointment tubes
  • Pesticide containers & chemical sprays
⚠️ Safety: Always wrap diapers and sanitary pads in newspaper marked with a red cross.

Understanding the 1 to 5 Star Segregation Rating

When the Waste Collector scans your QR code at your door, they record an audit rating based on compliance:

1
Completely Mixed

Wet and dry waste dumped into one container without segregation.

2
Poor Segregation

Plastics or glass mixed inside organic waste bucket.

3
Average Segregation

Separated bins, but sanitary waste or soiled food packets mixed.

4
Good Segregation

Wet and dry segregated cleanly; minimal contamination.

5
Pristine Model

Flawless 3-way segregation. Eligible for village Green Household Recognition!
