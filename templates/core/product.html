Module 01 • Identification Hardware

Batch Cryptographic Doorstep QR Engine

Zero Resident PII on Physical Tags: No citizen phone numbers, family names, or confidential details are printed on public-facing gate cards.

✓
Instant Server-Side Boundary Validation: A tag scanned outside its registered Panchayat is instantly flagged as unauthorized.

Module 02 • Field Frontline Operations

Zero-Hardware Mobile Collector Web PWA

Sanitation field personnel do not need expensive handheld hardware or proprietary barcode scanners. They simply open GreenPath in Chrome on their standard personal smartphone.

✓
HTML5 Camera Integration: Sub-second optical QR code decoding with audio-visual haptic confirmation upon successful gate match.
✓
Exception Logging: If a resident's house is locked or waste is refused, the collector logs the structured reason with one tap for municipal accountability.
✓
Daily Collector Route Counter: Real-time counter tracks progress against the total ward target, motivating collectors to complete 100% of their street route.
Module 03 • Waste Quality Index

3-Way Segregation Auditing & Quality Grading

Collecting unsegregated garbage turns landfills into environmental hazards. GreenPath turns every collection into a structured data point for source segregation.

✓
Wet / Dry / Sanitary Classification: Tracks presence of biodegradable kitchen organic waste, clean recyclables (paper/plastic), and sanitary/medical waste.
✓
1 to 5 Star Segregation Rating: Collectors rate household segregation compliance in real time. The cumulative score creates a transparent Segregation Index for the ward.
✓
Citizen Awareness Feedback Loop: Households see their score in their resident dashboard, fostering positive community hygiene habits.
