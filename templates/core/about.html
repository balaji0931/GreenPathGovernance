Our Civic Purpose

GreenPath was created to replace unverified paper logs and ghost entries with transparent, cryptographic digital governance — designed specifically for rural Gram Panchayats, peri-urban wards, and residential associations.

The Municipal Dilemma

Why Solid Waste Management Breaks Down at the Grassroots

Across thousands of rural and semi-urban habitations, significant public funds are dedicated to solid waste collection vehicles and personnel. Yet, local leaders and residents face persistent bottlenecks:

1. Phantom Visits & Paper Registers Without digital gate timestamps, paper registers are often back-filled with fictitious tick-marks, making it impossible to verify if collectors actually visited peripheral streets.
2. High Cost of Proprietary Hardware Traditional municipal tracking systems require ₹25,000+ rugged POS barcode guns that break in monsoon seasons and require costly proprietary maintenance contracts.
3. Mixed Waste Dumped in Open Yards Without doorstep segregation ratings, residents have zero incentive to separate wet food scraps from dry recyclables, turning community processing centers into hazardous open dumps.

Founding Philosophy

Four Pillars of the GreenPath Architecture

Privacy by Design

Public door tags carry only an anonymous UID. Household phone numbers, resident names, and contact details are stored securely on the server and never revealed to passersby.

Dignity of Sanitation Workers

Waste collectors are frontline civic heroes. Digital gate scanning provides irrefutable proof of their daily route completion, protecting them against false claims of missed visits.

Circular Economy First

Waste segregated at the source directly reduces landfill load. Organic waste can be composted in village pits, while dry recyclables generate legitimate secondary revenue for the Panchayat.

Zero Technical Barrier

Citizens do not need to download heavy apps or possess smartphones to benefit. The simple printed door tag works with any authorized collector scanner seamlessly.
