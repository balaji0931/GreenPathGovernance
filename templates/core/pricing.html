Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Why are households not charged directly?

Charging households directly creates friction, requires payment gateway transaction fees, and reduces citizen participation. By charging the managing organization (Gram Panchayat or RWA), the service remains 100% free for residents, ensuring 100% community adoption.

What is the ₹500 minimum floor?

The minimum floor covers server hosting, database maintenance, and continuous updates. It includes up to 100 households (₹5 × 100 = ₹500). If a village has 60 households, the charge is ₹500. If a village has 300 households, the charge is ₹1,500.

How do Gram Panchayats fund GreenPath?

Under the 15th Central Finance Commission (XV-FC) tied grants and the Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) Phase-II guidelines, Gram Panchayats have allocated budgets specifically for solid waste management monitoring, digital surveillance, and sanitation audits. GreenPath falls cleanly within sanctioned O&M expenditure limits.

Are there any hidden hardware setup costs?

Zero hardware costs! Waste collectors use their existing Android phones via our web app. Household QR tags are printed in-house using any standard office printer onto weatherproof sticker paper or cardstock.
